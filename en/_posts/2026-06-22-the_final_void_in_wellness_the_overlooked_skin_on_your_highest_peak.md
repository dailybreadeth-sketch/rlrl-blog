### 💡 FAQ: Optimizing Your Active Scalp Routine

### Q. Should I apply a scalp essence after a workout?
### A. Yes. Salt from sweat can dry out the scalp and weaken the barrier. After rinsing your scalp with lukewarm water post-workout, applying a lightweight essence before blow-drying calms the warm skin and restores essential hydration.

### Q. Can I just use my facial toner on my scalp?
### A. Facial skincare formulas often contain rich emollients that can weigh down hair roots or clog scalp pores. It is best to use a specialized, water-based botanical essence designed to absorb rapidly without leaving a greasy residue.
